Paper I • Target Journals: IEEE/Optica JOCN & IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management (TNSM)
Sub-50ms Autonomous Physical-Layer Remediation and Millimeter-Scale Fault Localization in Optical Networks Using Hybrid 1D-CNN/Transformer Models on 12-Bit Telemetry
Authors: Eng. Saleem Asseel, Batoul Mowafak Zain Alabdin, and Ahmad Jalal Assil
Presents an end-to-end closed-loop physical-layer telemetry and autonomous remediation architecture combining 12-bit native ADC sampling (1.25 GSa/s) with a hybrid 1D-CNN and Time-Series Transformer model. Achieves 98.4% fault classification accuracy, 0.015 m (1.5 cm) spatial localization RMSE, and 41.8 ms autonomous switch healing over Netmiko/SSH on Cisco IE-3000-8FM and MikroTik CRS305 hardware.

Paper II • Target Journals: IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement (TIM) & IEEE Sensors Journal
Design and Experimental Characterization of a 12-Bit Native ADC Four-Channel Synchronous Optical Reflectometer with Floating-Ground InGaAs PIN-TIA Front-End
Authors: Eng. Saleem Asseel, Ahmad Jalal Assil, and Batoul Mowafak Zain Alabdin
Details the metrological design and experimental validation of the CableHunt 12-Bit Native ADC (4,096 quantization steps) 4-channel reflectometer with floating-ground DC isolation, 50-Ω matched InGaAs PIN-TIA receiver (1100–1700 nm), and custom SFF-8472 SFP breakout board. Demonstrates a +24.08 dB quantization noise reduction (16× vertical resolution gain), Event Dead Zone (EDZ) < 0.8 m (0.75 m measured), and 85% front-end cost savings.

Paper III • Target Journals: IEEE/Optica Journal of Lightwave Technology (JLT) & Optics Express
Adaptive Multi-Band Spectral Optimization (1260–1675 nm) for 1G-to-40G Capacity Multiplication Over Legacy G.652 Single-Mode Optical Links
Authors: Eng. Saleem Asseel, Batoul Mowafak Zain Alabdin, and Ahmad Jalal Assil
Formulates and validates the multi-band spectral optimization objective across the O, E, S, C, L, and U bands (1260–1675 nm) using quasi-Newton L-BFGS-B optimization to bypass 1383 nm OH− water-peak absorption and Kerr nonlinearities, multiplying brownfield 1G G.652 links to 40G line-rates with 80%–88% CAPEX savings.
