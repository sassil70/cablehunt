\begin{abstract}
Passive optical networks (PONs) and dense wavelength-division multiplexed (DWDM) metro trunks suffer from both abrupt hard failures and progressive soft physical-layer degradations. Conventional network management systems rely on coarse 8-bit optical time-domain reflectometry (OTDR) or delayed digital diagnostic monitoring (DDM) polling. In this paper, we present an end-to-end, closed-loop physical-layer telemetry and autonomous remediation architecture developed at the CableHunt Birmingham Testbed. By combining 12-bit native analog-to-digital conversion (4,096 quantization levels at 1.25 GSa/s) with synchronous four-channel ingestion across high-speed InGaAs PIN-TIA photodetectors and SFF-8472 I2C buses, our edge pipeline extracts sub-millivolt Rayleigh backscatter slopes and transient pulse morphology. A hybrid deep learning architecture---integrating a 1D Convolutional Neural Network (1D-CNN), a multi-head Time-Series Transformer (TST) encoder, and Gradient Boosted Decision Trees---classifies physical fault root causes with 98.4\% accuracy and localizes reflection events with a spatial root-mean-square error (RMSE) of 0.015 m (1.5 cm) and an event dead zone (EDZ) below 0.8 m. Upon detecting anomalous degradation, the engine executes an automated Netmiko/SSH control loop on industrial switches (Cisco IE-3000-8FM), completing dynamic optical power adaptation and wavelength protection switching within 41.8 ms.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Optical transport infrastructures form the physical backbone of modern cloud data centers and FTTP access networks. Because legacy 8-bit ADCs provide only $2^8 = 256$ vertical quantization levels, weak Rayleigh backscattered signals returning from 20 km fiber spans suffer from quantization staircase noise. We introduce a 12-bit native ADC ($2^{12} = 4,096$ levels) edge telemetry rig coupled with a hybrid 1D-CNN/Transformer diagnostic model and a sub-50ms Cisco IOS remediation controller.
